Riverty card roadmap

Scored on Effort vs. Value for a team of four. Cards move between lanes with the arrow buttons.

Persona
Heavy German Riverty users who shop mostly online and use pay-later to receive the goods before paying.
Moment of misery
Payment timing and tracking don't follow what they've decided.
Primary metric
Share of activated cardholders who make a second card purchase within 30 days of their first.
Guardrail
Integrated checkout volume per heavy user at partner merchants. Card spend on the same purchases swaps a 1.54% merchant fee for 0.1% interchange.

High value means 4 or 5. Effort of 3 or more will not fit in one sprint for a team of four. Filtering dims the other cards.

Ideas left off the roadmap, with the research reason.

    Scores are product judgment, not engineering estimates. Evidence comes from 15 German user interviews and a survey of about 1,000 Riverty users (2023); the 2026 platform, risk and regulatory setup may change what is feasible.
