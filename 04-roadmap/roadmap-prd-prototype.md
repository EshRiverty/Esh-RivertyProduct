# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Heavy German Riverty users who shop mostly online and use pay-later mainly to receive the goods before paying.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** The share of activated cardholders who make a second card purchase within 30 days of their first.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Payment timing and tracking don't follow what they've decided.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Integrated checkout volume per heavy user at partner merchants. If users switch the same purchases onto the card, Riverty swaps a 1.54% merchant fee for 0.1% interchange, and contribution margin falls from 1.3% to −0.1% on that volume, per the deck's ball-park figures. This also protects the merchant-first positioning.

This goes straight at the moment of misery, that payment timing and tracking don't follow what users decided.
